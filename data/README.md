# Corpus data and licensing

Application MIT does not relicense upstream data. Inputs are pinned by exact revision/download digest and every file's SHA-256 in `manifests/sources.json`; bundled license notices have independent hashes checked when manifests load. `downloads/` and `generated/` are ignored build products. Full source downloads were authorized and completed, followed by the actual full import and streaming export. `audits/m1-full-import.json` records verified acceptance evidence; `generated/full-import-report.json` and `generated/storage/export-report.json` record local counts and storage sizes.

## Per-source audit, 2026-10-26

- **SBLGNT:** publisher repository [Faithlife/SBLGNT](https://github.com/Faithlife/SBLGNT) (the former LogosBible URL redirects), revision `d7f35adca9ec410e1d1499c63b4dd989d76c8d4d`. Every one of the 27 `data/sblgnt/text/*.txt` files is pinned and imported, including the upstream pericope-adulterae inclusion. CC BY 4.0 text license is bundled separately. Attribution: Michael W. Holmes, *The Greek New Testament: SBL Edition*, ©2010 Society of Biblical Literature and Logos Bible Software. Apparatus files are excluded; source verse punctuation/markers are retained.
- **Macula Greek:** [Clear-Bible/macula-greek](https://github.com/Clear-Bible/macula-greek), revision `8423afe47b9e8f24b7772e808af45c7159a6fe7e`, all 27 numbered `SBLGNT/lowfat/*.xml` books. CC BY 4.0 applies to morphology, syntax and participant referents; public-domain Berean `gloss` is included. UBS `ln/domain`, Cherith `english/mandarin`, and all unlisted attributes are excluded. Attribution and mixed-source notice: `licenses/macula.txt`. Morphology and referents are upstream analyses, not independently reviewed conclusions.
- **BSB:** full NT rows from the [publisher text download](https://bereanbible.com/bsb.txt), pinned by checksum. Publisher's initial notice dedicates text to the public domain; [licensing page](https://berean.bible/licensing.htm) dates dedication to April 30, 2023. All 7,957 NT slots are preserved, including 16 explicit empty upstream rows (`textStatus: empty-upstream`); there are 7,941 nonempty texts. OT rows are intentionally outside the M1 reader, although cross-reference targets may point there. Translation tables, reverse interlinear alignment and markup fields are not imported or licensed by assumption.
- **STEP TBESG:** [STEPBible/STEPBible-Data](https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data), revision `be4191f0082aeedddb0eff0c02c966292b2bd641`. Full `Lexicons/TBESG - Translators Brief lexicon of Extended Strongs for Greek - STEPBible.org CC BY.txt` acquired and imported. File-specific header grants CC BY 4.0 and identifies Abbott-Smith, Middle Liddell and STEP contributors. It defines lexical rows after a nonlexical proper-name metadata preamble; only the 11,035 tabular `G…` lexical rows are imported. Presentation HTML is converted to plain text with lexical wording retained. The importer rejects/omits `(MT)` Teknia definitions when present because a separate sublicensing grant was not established; the pinned TBESG file has zero such rows. STEP's edited lexicon and Abbott-Smith are related historical analyses, not two independent modern scholarly votes. TTESV and other tagged Bibles are excluded.
- **Abbott-Smith:** [translatable-exegetical-tools/Abbott-Smith](https://github.com/translatable-exegetical-tools/Abbott-Smith), revision `8c00cb244761aa23659421a8cbbbf7a3b27b7d59`. The project's own README explicitly places `abbott-smith.tei.xml`, including markup, in the public domain. All 6,153 TEI entries are imported, including 257 unnumbered irregular-verb appendix entries; main headwords use source `n` values, then orthography/Greek form fallback for appendix items. The PDF's separately noted restrictions are not transferred to the TEI and the PDF is excluded. Entry IDs, available Strong number and page locators are preserved; markup becomes plain text. Attribution and full notice in `licenses/abbott-smith.txt`.
- **STEP TFLSJ:** the two pinned full/extra files were acquired **for audit only**. Their headers identify a Perseus-derived full LSJ. Independent permissive redistribution rights for that digital edition were not established, so no TFLSJ definitions are exported. This source has explicit not-loaded coverage; Abbott-Smith supplies the second permissive lexicon. Do not infer that a repository-level license cures incompatible upstream terms.
- **OpenBible:** [OpenBible.info cross-reference page](https://www.openbible.info/labs/cross-references/) links the complete archive at `https://a.openbible.info/data/cross-references.zip`. The acquired archive is pinned by SHA-256 and contains only `From Verse`, `To Verse`, `Votes` and an attribution/date header. The page's license link resolves to CC BY 4.0. All 344,799 data rows are imported, including target ranges and negative votes. Attribution: OpenBible.info / Stephen Smith, primarily Treasury of Scripture Knowledge. ESV quotations and site scripture text are excluded. These editorial associations and votes do not establish a shared sense or historical dependence.
- **Grammar:** `manifests/grammar-sources.json` pins the exact original-edition OCR used to verify Smyth (1920, University of Michigan scan) and Robertson (1914, Princeton scan). `grammar/entries.json` contains ten original application explanations and verified page/section links; no invented quotations. Underlying originals are public domain in the US. Full scans/OCR and Perseus/Alpheios NC/SA markup are excluded from redistribution. See `licenses/grammars.txt`. Robertson's pp. 683–684 explicitly connect Matthew 28:19 αὐτούς to ἔθνη under pronouns according to sense; this does not decide corporate versus individual exegesis.
- **reformed-retrieval:** read-only [upstream](https://github.com/kadencartwright/reformed-retrieval), revision `808956974894a625f84160761bc3fa9852d662e1`; exact `MANIFEST.sha256` digest pinned in `manifests/reformed-retrieval.json`. Every member is verified, including excluded originals. Only the explicitly CC0 `completed/*/english.md` and `completed/*/parts/*-english.md` paths are imported. Normalized Latin/French, metadata and all uncompleted English are outside that dedication and remain linked upstream. Inventory enumerates all pinned work paths with imported/omitted reasons, including 28 in-progress projects; every excerpt remains AI-assisted/unreviewed. Compiled and part translations may duplicate citations. No author's silence is inferred from absent, untranslated or unindexed material.

`samples/` holds genuine excerpts for fast tests, not fabricated scholarship. Selected lexical samples are exact source rows/TEI entries; the TEI wrapper is newly added to make the excerpt a well-formed document. Synthetic parser/filter cases exist only in tests and are labeled there. The full import always reads complete pinned inputs. No UBS SA, NC, commercial Bible text or ESV quotations are in the core export.

## M2–M4 audited pack extension

The preceding audit describes the base M1 import. `manifests/milestone-packs.json` now pins all files for separately selected core, SA and NC packs. Copied notices are under `licenses/milestones/`. See [current exact counts, fields and exclusions](../docs/milestones-local.md#data-rights-and-scholarly-boundaries). The extension imports BSB publisher tables and OT text, WEB, OSHB/Macula Hebrew, BDB/Strong, Westminster Standards, UBS dictionaries/domains and native LXX. BSB table columns for variant comparisons/NA/ECM and notes remain excluded; Macula Hebrew excludes UBS domain fields. The LXX's upstream NC-SA notice remains controlling despite its converter's MIT wrapper. Private original transcriptions are local-only, never checked in.

Acquisition checks every selected input against its stored SHA-256. Git sources use exact revisions; mutable publisher URLs fail closed on digest drift. `milestone-packs.py --audit` is a deliberate source-audit operation. Ordinary application builds do not acquire corpora or opt into additional licenses.
