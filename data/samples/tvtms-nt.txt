NOTES: 												
* The left side is the Source ref and this must be unique, so "!a" etc is added. The right side is the Standard (KJV) ref and does not need to be unique, but "!a" etc is added to help make it humanly readable 												
* These mappings are only added when particular circumstances are true, listed as Tests in the data lines below. 												
* The OT is almost always uniform with KJV versification in the English Bibles, but there is great variety in the OT and NT in Bibles influenced by Hebrew, Greek and Latin traditions. 												
* Occasionally it is better to leave versification uncorrected, as with ESV Rev.12.17. The difference  is equivalent to moving the verse division by a few words, and a correction will merely add confusion.
